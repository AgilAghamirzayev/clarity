# Clarity: three-minute presentation

Eight slides. Target: 180 seconds including short pauses and slide changes. Use Next slide manually. The time shown beside the slider is animation time, not speaking time. The suggested speaking time appears below each slide.

## 1. Clarity (0:00-0:10)

Clarity turns customer conversations into evidence your team can act on. Let me show you how one recorded call becomes a reviewed business action.

## 2. Problem and solution (0:10-0:30)

Contact centers capture valuable feedback every day, but reviewing recordings takes time. Similar complaints remain scattered, and follow-up is difficult to track. Clarity connects those pieces: the recording, the customer issue, the supporting evidence, and the action a team decides to take.

## 3. Capture (0:30-1:00)

Imagine a customer saying, “My payment failed twice.” Your existing contact center records the conversation. Once the source is connected, it sends the completed audio and call details to Clarity. Clarity validates the recording and turns speech into a timestamped transcript. We now have readable evidence, linked to the exact moment in the original call, without asking someone to summarize each recording manually.

## 4. Understand (1:00-1:30)

Next, Clarity masks sensitive identifiers in the transcript, such as a name or phone number. The original audio stays unchanged. Your selected AI analyzes the masked text using your company context and rules. In this example, it identifies a repeated payment failure and links that finding to Call 001 at twelve seconds. The team can check the evidence instead of accepting an unsupported AI statement.

## 5. Connect (1:30-2:05)

One call tells us about one customer. Related calls can reveal a recurring problem. Here, three illustrative customers describe similar payment failures. Clarity groups their findings while keeping the individual evidence linked. It then proposes a next step: investigate the failed attempts and payment error responses. This is a recommendation to investigate, not a claim that we already know the cause. Your team sees both the proposal and why it was suggested.

## 6. Act and learn (2:05-2:40)

A person reviews the evidence, approves or rejects the proposal, and assigns an owner. Approved work can go through configured Jira, Slack or CRM integrations. The public demo simulates those deliveries. Afterward, the team tracks progress and compares issue trends before and after the action. That helps decide whether to continue, adjust, or investigate further. The important connection is evidence, human decision, owned action, and an observed result.

## 7. Team (2:40-2:50)

Our four-person team brings together software engineering and applied AI experience. We are building Clarity around the way support and operations teams actually work.

## 8. Thank you (2:50-3:00)

Clarity helps teams understand customer calls, see recurring issues, and make accountable decisions. Thank you. We would be happy to show the workflow in action.
