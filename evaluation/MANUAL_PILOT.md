# Manual versus assisted pilot

Status: **pending**. No interviews, consented customer recordings or business outcomes have been collected for this evaluation.

## Next pilot

Recruit two consenting support reviewers. Start with the existing synthetic recordings to test the task and instructions. Agree on a lawful, consented recording source before using customer calls. Obtain approval for retention, access and withdrawal; keep identities outside evaluation exports.

Prepare two matched sets of calls, balanced by duration, issue difficulty, language and audio quality. Each reviewer processes one set manually and the other with Clarity. Reverse the order for the second reviewer. Do not show a reviewer the same call in both conditions. Record assignment and exclusions before the session. Two reviewers are a usability pilot, not a powered business-impact study.

Task: identify customer problems, cite supporting speech, propose a justified next step and decide whether the item needs investigation. The assisted condition can inspect the transcript and recording, edit findings and reject recommendations. Preserve normal human approval. Do not create external tickets or send messages during the study.

Measure active review time, time to first useful finding, issue precision/recall against an independently agreed reference, unsupported recommendations, corrections, overlooked PII and confidence. Record tool waiting time separately. Capture whether a reviewer accepted an incorrect suggestion. Use a short interview afterward to understand confusion and perceived usefulness; do not treat satisfaction as evidence of accuracy.

Have two reviewers label issues, evidence spans and supported actions independently, before viewing model results. Resolve disagreements with an adjudicator and retain the original labels. For real recordings, use a restricted artifact location and pseudonymous IDs. Review the generated `semantic-review-pending.csv` only after reading the source case and model evidence.
